# Roastly

Should this name be taken, or fall through, I will call this application **BLOOM**.

## The Coffee Culture App for Everyone

Roastly is a next-generation, first of its kind social platform.

Designed originally as a database of specialty coffee houses, Roastly brings the latest and greatest cafes to the people who love them the most. 

## How it works

Roastly is designed to be *ergonomic, performant, and simple.*

Users can easily sign up, favorite cafes, and see what cafes are the latest obsession for the people they know.

For a small premium, users will be able to access Roastly's proprietary **TasteMap** algorithm, which will let them build a dynamic profile of their personal coffee preference, and laser in on the spots that they'll love the most.
